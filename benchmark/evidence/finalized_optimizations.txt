   Gain: 1.245x transformer speedup; 19.7% less transformer time.
   Full generation: 267.065 s -> 237.764 s; 29.301 s saved (11.0% less request time).

6. Cache identical padded VAE conditioning encodes within the request
   Each 49-frame window can present the same padded conditioning tensor. Encode it once, reuse its latent only after exact tensor equality, and preserve the VAE cache reset on each hit.
   Main 463-frame run: 11 cache hits and 1 full padded encode; the 12 shorter motion encodes still run. Full generation 176.309 s -> 158.016 s. This saves 18.293 s (10.4% less request time; 1.116x speedup) on top of steps 1-5.
   Four validation samples (main, s01, s03, s06) produced final videos with SHA-256 hashes identical to their corresponding step-5 videos. PSNR versus step 5 is infinite and SSIM is 1.0; quality versus the original Q8 baseline is unchanged from step 5.
   Benchmark setting: VAE_OPT_MODE=cache_identical_encode in run_canonical.py; the source worker is unchanged. The cache retains one padded input and latent in GPU memory; production integration remains to be done.
